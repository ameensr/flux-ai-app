# Team Capacity 3D Modal - User Guide

## What's New? 🎉

The **Capacity Distribution** widget in your QA Weekly Report Dashboard is now interactive! Click on it to open a beautiful 3D card modal with detailed team capacity insights.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Find the Capacity Distribution Widget
Look for the "Capacity Distribution" card in the Team Capacity section of your report. It displays a colorful donut chart showing your team's availability.

### Step 2: Hover to See the Indicator
When you hover your mouse over the widget, you'll see:
- A subtle glow effect around the card
- A "Click to Expand" badge appears in the top-right corner
- The border changes to a green tint
- The card slightly lifts with a shadow effect

### Step 3: Click to Open
Click anywhere on the Capacity Distribution card to open the detailed 3D modal.

### Step 4: Explore the Details
The modal contains:

#### 📊 **Distribution Chart**
- Large pie chart showing capacity breakdown
- Total team members count
- Color-coded segments for each status

#### 📈 **Status Breakdown**
- **Available** (Green): Team members working this week
- **On Leave** (Yellow): Team members on vacation/leave
- **No Logs** (Red): Team members with no logged hours
- Each status shows count and percentage

#### 📉 **Capacity Metrics**
Four key metrics at a glance:
- Total Members
- Available Members
- Average Working Hours
- Overall Capacity Percentage

#### 👥 **Team Availability Table**
Detailed breakdown of each team member:
- Employee name
- Logged hours
- Leave hours
- Status badge (Available, On Leave, or No Logs)

#### 💡 **Capacity Insights**
Smart recommendations based on your team's capacity:
- **Strong Capacity (≥80%)**: Green indicator - Team is operating well
- **Moderate Capacity (60-79%)**: Yellow indicator - Some constraints
- **Reduced Capacity (<60%)**: Red indicator - Consider workload adjustments

### Step 5: Scroll Within the Modal
- The main page is locked when the modal is open
- You can scroll inside the modal to see all details
- The main page stays in place in the background

### Step 6: Close the Modal
Three ways to close:
1. Click the **X button** in the top-right corner (with rotate animation)
2. Click anywhere on the **dark backdrop** outside the modal
3. Press the **Escape key** (if keyboard navigation is enabled)

When you close:
- The modal smoothly animates out with a 3D effect
- The main page scroll is restored to your previous position
- You can continue browsing your report

## Visual Features ✨

### Animations
- **Entrance**: Modal slides up with a 3D rotate effect (spring animation)
- **Exit**: Modal slides down with a reverse 3D rotate
- **Hover**: Interactive elements respond to mouse hover
- **Charts**: Animated pie chart segments grow smoothly

### Colors & Effects
- **Green glow**: Represents team availability
- **Blue glow**: Adds depth to the design
- **Gradient overlays**: Professional glass-morphism effect
- **Pulsing indicators**: Shows live data status

### Responsive Design
- **Desktop**: Full-width layout with side-by-side columns
- **Tablet**: Stacked layout with adjusted spacing
- **Mobile**: Single column, optimized for touch

## Scroll Behavior 🔒

### When Modal is CLOSED
- Normal page scrolling works as usual
- You can scroll through your entire report

### When Modal is OPEN
- **Main page scroll is LOCKED** ❌
- **Only modal content scrolls** ✅
- Main page stays fixed in the background
- Your scroll position is preserved

### When Modal CLOSES
- Main page scroll is unlocked
- You return to the exact position you were at
- No jumps or layout shifts

## Data Sources

The modal shows different information based on your data:

### With Uploaded File
- Displays file name at the top
- Shows period start and end dates
- Includes all team member details from the file

### Manual Entry
- Shows "Team Availability Overview"
- Displays manually entered capacity data
- Same visualizations and metrics

## Troubleshooting

### Modal won't open?
- Make sure you have team capacity data
- Check that JavaScript is enabled
- Try refreshing the page

### Scroll not working?
- The modal should scroll internally
- If stuck, close and reopen the modal
- Clear browser cache if issue persists

### Animation is laggy?
- Close other browser tabs
- Check system resources
- Try a different browser

### Can't close the modal?
- Click the X button in top-right
- Click outside the modal on the dark area
- Refresh the page as a last resort
